# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Chalkwright welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Contributors
should assume good intent, discuss technical trade-offs with evidence, respect
privacy and safety boundaries, and make space for people with different levels
of experience.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- protecting students, operators, and maintainers from unnecessary exposure;
- keeping disagreement focused on ideas and evidence; and
- helping others understand unfamiliar parts of the system.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
misrepresentation, disclosure of private information, credential or student-
data sharing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, project communication, and public spaces where someone represents
Chalkwright.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer using a private
contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not disclose
sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, close participation, or restrict access when necessary to protect the
community. Reports will be handled as privately and proportionately as
practical.
